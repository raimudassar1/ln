You are creating one language-learning content pack for beginner to early B1 learners.

Target language: [TARGET LANGUAGE]
Learner level: [Novice / A1 / A2 / Early B1]
Pack number: [PACK NUMBER]
Pack title: [TITLE]
Core topic: [TOPIC]
Story situation: [STORY DESCRIPTION]
Vocabulary source: Use only the vocabulary already available in this pack. Do not introduce too many advanced words. If new words are necessary, keep them minimal and explain them clearly.
Grammar focus: [GRAMMAR POINT]
Communication goal: [WHAT THE LEARNER SHOULD BE ABLE TO SAY]
Tone: natural, simple, useful for real life, not childish unless the pack is for kids.

Create the following content:

1. Pack Title
2. Short Story Introduction
   - 4 to 6 simple sentences explaining the situation.
   - Make the story realistic and connected to daily life.

3. Main Dialogue
   - 2 speakers minimum.
   - 10 to 16 lines for Novice/A1.
   - 16 to 24 lines for A2.
   - 24 to 32 lines for early B1.
   - Use natural conversation.
   - Keep sentences short at beginner levels.
   - Recycle vocabulary several times.
   - Include greetings, questions, answers, small reactions, and a clear ending.

4. Dialogue Translation
   - Translate line by line into English if the target language is not English.
   - If the target language is English, provide simple learner-friendly explanations instead.

5. Key Sentences
   - 8 useful sentences from the dialogue.
   - Include meaning and one extra example for each.

6. Vocabulary Practice
   - 12 to 20 vocabulary items from the pack.
   - Include word, meaning, part of speech, and example sentence.

7. Speaking Practice
   - Part A: Repeat-after-me sentences.
   - Part B: Role-play with missing information.
   - Part C: Personal answer questions.

8. Listening Practice
   - 5 comprehension questions.
   - 5 true/false questions.
   - 5 fill-in-the-blank questions using dialogue sentences.

9. Writing Practice
   - Beginner: write 3 to 5 sentences.
   - A1: write a short message of 40 to 60 words.
   - A2: write a short paragraph of 70 to 100 words.
   - Early B1: write a 100 to 140 word response, opinion, or short story.

10. Mini Grammar Section
   - Explain the grammar focus in very simple language.
   - Give 5 examples.
   - Give 5 practice questions.

11. Expansion Dialogue
   - Create a second shorter dialogue in a slightly different situation.
   - Use the same vocabulary and grammar.

12. Review Task
   - Give one speaking task, one writing task, and one self-check question.

Important rules:
- Do not create random textbook sentences with no purpose.
- Make the pack useful for real communication.
- Each pack must have a unique situation and not repeat the same story.
- The story should gradually become more complex as the pack number increases.
- Keep the language level appropriate.
